description: Ask questions about the Uprevit documentation, and see exactly what Ask AI can and cannot access.

**Ask AI** answers questions about this documentation. It reads the documentation pages, writes an answer, and links to the pages it used.

<Callout type="info" title="Ask AI never sees your organization's data">
  Ask AI only knows the Uprevit documentation. It has no access to your workspace, departments, projects, products, source files, approval workflows, exports, or user accounts.
</Callout>

## Ask a question

1. Open any documentation page.
2. Click **Ask AI**, or press **⌘ /** on Mac or **Ctrl /** on Windows.
3. Type your question, or pick one of the suggested questions.

Each answer links to the documentation pages it came from, so you can check the full explanation.

## Ask about selected text

1. Select a sentence or paragraph on a documentation page.
2. Click the **Ask AI** button that appears next to your selection.
3. Ask your question. The selected text is added to the chat as context.

## What Ask AI can access

| Sent with your question | Never sent |
|-------------------------|------------|
| The question you type | Workspace, department, project, and product data |
| Earlier messages in the same chat | Source files, symbols, and images |
| The title and address of the documentation page you're on | Approval workflows, redlines, reports, and exports |
| Documentation text you select and add to the chat | User accounts, names, and email addresses |
| The Uprevit documentation itself | Anything else stored in your workspace |

Ask AI runs on the documentation pages only. It doesn't connect to your workspace, so it cannot read, change, or share your organization's data.

<Callout type="warn" title="Don't paste confidential information">
  Ask AI sends whatever you type to an AI model to write the answer. Keep questions about how Uprevit works, and don't paste product details, customer information, or other confidential data into the chat.
</Callout>

## Where your chats are saved

Your recent chats are saved in your browser on this device, so you can go back to them from the chat history. They aren't saved to your workspace and aren't visible to other users. Clearing your browser data removes them.

## Who can use Ask AI

Ask AI is available to signed-in Uprevit users on the documentation pages.
