A **workspace** is the top-level container for everything in Uprevit. One workspace represents your company or organization. All departments, projects, products, users, and source files belong to a single workspace.

<DocsVideo videoKey="getting-started.workspace" title="Workspace overview" />

## What lives in a workspace

| Concept | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Departments** | Organizational groupings (e.g. Regulatory, Packaging). |
| **Projects** | Work streams within a department. |
| **Products** | Labeling documentation records tied to a department and project. |
| **Users** | Members invited to collaborate in the workspace. |
| **Source files** | Shared folder tree for reference documents. |

## Dashboard

The **Dashboard** (`/dashboard`) gives you a quick view of the workspace:

- **Stat cards** for Departments, Projects, Products, Source Files, and Archives. Each card also shows how many were active, or archived, in the last 30 days.
- **Departments** and **Projects** cards with the most recent items, and **Show all** links to the full lists.
- A **products table** with the most recently changed products. Switch between **Show all products** and **Show products owned by me**.

## Navigation

The app sidebar is grouped into sections:

| Section | Pages |
|---------|-------|
| **Workspace** | Dashboard, Departments, Projects, Products, Workflows |
| **Library** | Source Files, Bookmarked Products, Archive |
| **Insights** | Reports, Analytics |
| **Help** | Documentation, Contact Support, System Status |

The bell in the top bar opens your [notifications](/docs/review-outputs/notifications).

## Open Settings

Open **Settings** from the workspace menu at the bottom of the sidebar, or from your avatar menu.

The tabs you see depend on your role:

| Tab | Who sees it | What it's for |
|-----|-------------|---------------|
| **Profile** | Everyone | Your own details and sign out. |
| **Workspace** | Admins | Workspace details, approval workflow defaults, and support settings. |
| **Users** | Everyone | Everyone in the workspace. |
| **Admins** | Admins | Workspace admins. |
| **Activity** | Admins | Every recorded change in the workspace, with filters and a CSV export. |
| **Usage** | Admins and Managers | Usage this billing period and usage limits. |
| **Billing** | Managers | Plan, current term, included seats, and invoices. |

If you open a link to a tab you don't have access to, Uprevit shows your **Profile** tab instead.

You can also switch between light and dark themes from the Settings header.

## Your profile

The **Profile** tab shows your **Personal Information**.

1. Click **Edit Profile**.
2. Update **Full Name**, **Email Address**, or **Role / Designation** (all required), and **Unit**, **Location**, or **Phone Number** (optional).
3. Optionally click **Upload Image** to add a profile picture: PNG, JPEG, JPG, GIF, or WebP, under 500 KB.
4. Click **Save Changes**.

Click **Sign Out** to sign out of Uprevit.

## Workspace details (admins)

<Callout type="warn" title="Admin only">
  Only admins can see the **Workspace** tab and edit workspace details.
</Callout>

The **Workspace** tab shows:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Workspace ID** | System identifier. Click the copy button to copy it. |
| **Workspace Name** | Display name for your organization in Uprevit. |
| **Company Name** | Legal or commercial company name. |
| **Plan** | Shows **NA** for now. |
| **User Count** | Number of members in the workspace. |
| **Workspace Description** | Short summary of the workspace. |

The workspace logo appears next to the workspace name.

To update workspace details:

1. Go to **Settings → Workspace**.
2. Click **Edit Workspace**.
3. Update **Workspace Name**, **Company Name**, or **Workspace Description**. All three are required. The description can be up to 220 characters.
4. Optionally upload or replace the **Workspace Logo**: PNG, JPEG, JPG, GIF, or WebP, under 500 KB.
5. Click **Save Changes**.

The **Workspace** tab also has two admin settings cards:

- **Approval Workflows**: choose the **Default workflow completion** for new workflows, **Automatic** or **Initiator-controlled**. See [Approval workflows](/docs/review-outputs/approval-workflows).
- **Support**: turn **Session replay for support** on or off. See [Contact Support](/docs/support#session-replay).

The workspace is created during first-time setup. The admin who sets it up enters the workspace name, their full name, company name, and an optional logo and description.

## Members

The **Users** tab lists everyone in the workspace, with their **User Type** (**Admin**, **User**, or **Approver**), a **Manager** badge for people with billing access, and their **Status**:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Active** | Has joined the workspace. |
| **Invited** | Has been invited but hasn't finished setting up their profile. |
| **Removed** | No longer has access. |

Use the filter and sort controls to find people by name, email, designation, location, status, or user type.

Admins also see the **Admins** tab, which lists only workspace admins. On both tabs, admins can turn on **Show removed** to include removed members.

## Member access

| Access | What they can do | Seat |
|--------|------------------|------|
| **Admin** | Manages the workspace, its members, and usage. Everything a User can do. | Full seat |
| **User** | Creates and edits Products and starts workflows. | Full seat |
| **Approver** | Views everything and acts only in workflows they're assigned to. | Approver seat |
| **Manager** | Sees usage, billing, and invoices. Can be added to any of the above, or given on its own. | Free when given on its own |

A **Manager only** member sees billing and usage but has no access to Products or workflows.

## Invite members (admins)

1. In **Settings**, click **Invite Users**.
2. Enter a **Name** and **Email** for each person, then choose their **Access**: **User**, **User + Manager**, **Approver**, **Approver + Manager**, or **Manager only**. Click **Add another** to add more, up to 10 at a time.
3. Click **Send Invitations**.

Invited people sign in and complete their profile before they can use the workspace. Inviting someone who was removed gives them access again. You can't invite someone as an Admin.

## Change member access (admins)

1. On the **Users** or **Admins** tab, click the person's access badge.
2. In **Change access**, pick the new access. For an Admin, choose **Admin** or **Admin + Manager**.
3. Click **Save access**.

To make someone an Approver or Manager only, first move them off any Products they own or contribute to, and any active workflows they started. For Manager only, also replace them as an approver on active workflows. **Change access** lists these with links, and **Check again** refreshes the list.

If your workspace blocks usage over its limits, an access change that would go over a seat limit isn't saved.

## Remove members (admins)

1. On the **Users** or **Admins** tab, click the remove icon next to the person.
2. Type their name to confirm.
3. Click **Remove**.

They lose access immediately. Their past activity in the workspace is kept. If they owned products, ownership moves to you.

## Workspace activity (admins)

**Settings → Activity** lists every recorded change in the workspace: products, projects, departments, source files, workflows, member access, and workspace settings. Filter by search text, action, record type, person, and date.

**Export CSV** queues a file of the 10,000 most recent events that match the current filters. It appears in the same panel when ready and is available for 24 hours. Member and workspace-settings events are visible to admins only.

## Usage and limits (admins and managers)

The **Usage** tab shows **Usage & limits** for the current billing period:

- **Full seats** (Admins and Users), **Approver seats**, **Exports**, and **Upload volume**, compared with your limits. Manager-only members are free and don't use a seat.
- Whether the **SSO add-on** is on the subscription. Built-in SAML/OIDC sign-in is coming soon — contact Uprevit support to plan it.

Under **Limit enforcement**, choose what happens when the workspace reaches a limit:

- **Allow overage**: exports, uploads, and new members keep working past the limit.
- **Block when over limit**: exports, uploads, and member changes that go over the limit are blocked.

You can also set an **Export limit** and an **Upload limit (GB)**, then click **Save preferences**. **Full seat** and **Approver seat** limits come from your subscription and can't be changed here. When blocking is on, Uprevit checks them when an invited member finishes setting up their profile and when a member's access changes.

## Billing and invoices (managers)

<Callout type="warn" title="Manager only">
  Only members with **Manager** access see the **Billing** tab and invoices. Admins can see it too once they're given **Admin + Manager** access.
</Callout>

The **Billing** tab shows:

| Section | What it shows |
|---------|---------------|
| **Plan** | Your plan, whether you're billed monthly or yearly, and the subscription status. |
| **Current term** | When the current term starts and ends, and the next billing date. |
| **Included** | Your **Full seats**, **Approver seats**, and whether SSO is included. |
| **References** | Your **Customer ID** and **Subscription ID**. Share these with Uprevit support when you ask about a bill. |
| **Invoices** | Every invoice for the workspace. Click **Refresh** to reload them. |

Click an invoice to see its line items and totals, and click **Download PDF** to save it. Click **Billing** in the header to go back. To change your plan, contact Uprevit support.

## Admin role

Your organization decides who is an admin when it sets up your sign-in account. There's no setting in Uprevit to make someone an admin. If you need admin access, ask an existing workspace admin.

## Typical setup flow (admins)

1. Create the workspace during first-time setup.
2. Invite team members from **Settings**.
3. Create **departments**, then **projects** under each department.
4. Team members create **products** under the appropriate project.

See [Departments](/docs/getting-started/departments), [Projects](/docs/getting-started/projects), and [Products](/docs/getting-started/products) for the next steps.
