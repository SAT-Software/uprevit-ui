title: Contact Support
description: Send a message to the Uprevit team, follow your conversations, and reply to the team from the app.

Use **Contact Support** to report a bug, request a feature, or ask the Uprevit team a question. Each message you send starts a **conversation**. Replies from the team appear in the app, and you can answer them from the same place.

## Open support

- In the app, click **Contact Support** in the **Help** section of the sidebar.
- In the documentation, click **Send feedback** at the bottom of the sidebar.

Both open the same **Send feedback** dialog, with two tabs: **New message** and **Your conversations**.

## Send a message

1. Open the **New message** tab.
2. Check **Name** and **Email**. They're filled in from your account, and both are required. The Uprevit team may also reply to this email address.
3. Choose a **Category**: **Bug**, **Feature**, or **Other**.
4. Optionally add a **Title**, up to 150 characters.
5. Describe the issue or idea in **Message**, up to 5,000 characters. For a bug, say what happened, what you expected, and how to reproduce it.
6. Optionally add screenshots.
7. Click **Send message**.

When the message is sent, you see **Message sent**. Click **View conversations** to follow it, or **Send another** to write a new message.

Your message includes the address of the page you were on, so the team can find the problem faster.

## Add screenshots

Click **Add image** to attach screenshots to a new message.

- Up to 4 images per message.
- PNG, JPEG, GIF, or WebP.
- Up to 5 MB each.

Only the images you add are sent. Uprevit never captures your screen automatically. Screenshots can only be opened by the Uprevit support team.

Replies can't include images. To share more screenshots, send a new message.

## View your conversations

Open the **Your conversations** tab to see every conversation you started, newest first. Each one shows its latest message, when it was last updated, and its status. Conversations with unread replies show how many replies are new.

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **New** | The team hasn't picked up your message yet. |
| **Open** | The team is working on it. |
| **Waiting on you** | The team replied and needs an answer from you. |
| **On hold** | The conversation is paused. |
| **Resolved** | The team closed the conversation. |

The list checks for new replies while the dialog is open. Older conversations load when you click **Load older conversations**.

## Reply to the team

1. Open a conversation from **Your conversations**.
2. Read the thread. Your messages are labeled **You**. Team messages show the team member's name.
3. Type in **Add a reply…**, then click **Send reply**.

Opening a conversation marks its replies as read. Click **All conversations** to go back to the list.

A **Resolved** conversation stays readable, but you can't reply to it. If you need more help, send a new message from **New message**.

## Session replay

Workspace admins can turn on session replay so the Uprevit team can see what happened when someone reports a problem.

1. Go to **Settings → Workspace**.
2. In the **Support** card, turn on **Session replay for support**.

While it's on, Uprevit records a masked replay of activity for signed-in members of the workspace. Text, form inputs, images, and documents are hidden in the replay. Replay is never recorded on support pages.

Only workspace admins can see the **Workspace** tab and change this setting. It's off by default.

## Who can use support

Every signed-in workspace member can send messages, view their own conversations, and reply. You only see conversations you started.
