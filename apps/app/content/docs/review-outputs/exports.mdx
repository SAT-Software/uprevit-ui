Uprevit creates PDFs and report spreadsheets as **background exports**, so you can keep working while the file is prepared. Specification grids download a spreadsheet **immediately**.

## Product PDF exports

### Start an export

From a product, do one of the following:

- In the product header, click the **Export product as PDF** icon.
- On `/products`, open the row menu and choose **Export to PDF**.

The **Export Product** dialog lists the sections included in the PDF: Product Information, Compliance Information, Label Components, Symbols & Graphics, Product Data, Operational Parameters, and Label Tags. Click **Queue PDF Export**.

While your export is being prepared, the product shows **Export in progress** and you can't edit it. Click **View Exports** to follow the export. Editing resumes when it finishes.

### Track and download

Click **Exports** on `/products` to open **Product Exports**. It lists your product exports, newest first, and you can filter by status:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Queued** | Waiting to start. |
| **Processing** | The file is being created. |
| **Ready** | Click the download icon to save the file. |
| **Failed** | The export didn't finish. The error is shown on the job. |

A message appears when an export is ready, even if you've moved to another page. Uprevit retries failed exports automatically before marking them **Failed**. If an export keeps failing, [contact support](/docs/support).

Exports are kept for **24 hours**. After that, start a new export.

## Report exports

From **Reports**:

1. Run a query.
2. In the results header, click **PDF** or **Excel**.
3. Click **Start PDF Export** or **Start Excel Export**.

Report exports include every product that matches your query, not just the page you're viewing, up to 1,000 products.

Click **Exports** on `/reports` to open **Report Exports**. Report exports use the same statuses and the same 24-hour limit as product exports.

## Immediate specification export

On **Product Specifications** and **Operational Parameters**, click **Export this table** in the grid toolbar. The current grid downloads straight away as an Excel file named `product-data-` followed by today's date. It doesn't use the background queue.

Use the product PDF export when you need a packaged document across tabs.

## Export formats

| Format | Where |
|--------|-------|
| **PDF** | A product's full documentation, or a report's results |
| **Excel** | A report's results, or a Product Specifications or Operational Parameters grid |

## Permissions

Every workspace member can export. You only see and download exports you started.

Exports count toward your workspace's export limit. If your admin has set exports to be blocked over the limit, new exports are refused until usage is back under it. See [Usage and limits](/docs/getting-started/workspace#usage-and-limits-admins).
