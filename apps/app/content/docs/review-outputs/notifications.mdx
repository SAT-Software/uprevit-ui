Uprevit notifies you when something needs your attention: you join a product team, someone asks you to approve, a decision is made, or reviewed content changes. Every notification appears in the app and is also sent to your email.

## Check your notifications

The bell in the top bar shows how many notifications you haven't read.

1. Click the bell to see your 6 most recent notifications.
2. Click a notification, or **View all notifications**, to open the **Notifications** page. **Mark all read** marks every notification as read, not just the ones shown.

The **Notifications** page (`/notifications`) has two tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
|-----|---------------|
| **All** | Every notification, newest first. |
| **Unread** | Only notifications you haven't opened. |

On this page, click a notification to open the product or workflow it's about. Opening a notification marks it as read. Click **Mark all as read** to mark every notification read at once. Click **Load more** to see older ones.

Unread notifications appear in bold, with a dot. New notifications show up within about a minute, without reloading the page.

## What you're notified about

### Product team

| Event | Who is notified |
|-------|-----------------|
| You're made the Product Owner of a product | The new owner |
| You're added as a contributor on a product | The new contributor |
| Someone else returns your version to Draft | The version's Product Owner |
| Products move to you because their owner was removed from the workspace | The person who removed the member |

### Approval workflows

| Event | Who is notified |
|-------|-----------------|
| A workflow starts | Every approver, plus the Product Owners of included products who aren't approvers |
| You're added as a replacement approver | The new approver |
| You're replaced as an approver | The approver who was replaced |
| An approver approves | The Initiator |
| An approver requests changes | The Initiator and the product team |
| A change request is marked addressed | The person who requested the change |
| Reviewed content changes during review | Approvers, so they can check the change before or after deciding |
| An approver leaves the workspace or the product team | The Initiator |
| The Initiator sends a reminder | The approvers the Initiator selects |
| All approvals are in and the workflow is ready to complete | The Initiator, when the workflow is Initiator-controlled |
| A workflow is completed, rejected, or cancelled | The Initiator, every approver, and every Product Owner |

You usually aren't notified about your own actions. The exception is removing a member: if their products move to you, you're notified. Workflow comments don't send notifications. Only change requests and their answers do.

## Email notifications

Each notification is also sent to the email address on your profile. The subject is the notification title, and **Open in Uprevit** takes you to the right page.

You can't turn notification emails off.

## Send a reminder

If you started a workflow, you can remind approvers who haven't decided yet:

1. Open the workflow.
2. Click **Send Reminder**.
3. Choose which approvers to remind. Approvers who haven't decided, or who requested changes, are selected for you.
4. Optionally add a **Message**, then click **Send Reminder**.

The approvers you choose get an in-app and email reminder.
