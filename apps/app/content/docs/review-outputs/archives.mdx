title: Archives
description: Archive and restore departments, projects, and products.
---

The **Archive** page (`/archive`) is where you browse entities removed from active lists and restore them when appropriate. Archiving is different from the product lifecycle. Archiving a product hides the whole product from active work without changing the status of any of its versions.

## Archive tabs

| Tab | What it lists |
|-----|----------------|
| **Departments** | Archived departments. |
| **Projects** | Archived projects. |
| **Products** | Archived products. |

Each table shows who archived each item and when, and you can filter and sort by **Archived By** and **Archived On**.

Any signed-in user can open `/archive` and browse tables. **Restore** and archive **creation** are admin-restricted (below).

## Archiving departments

<Callout type="warn" title="Admin only">
Archiving a department requires **Admin** privileges.
</Callout>

1. Open the department.
2. Choose **Archive**.
3. Type the **exact department name** to confirm, then click **Archive**.
4. The department disappears from active lists and appears under **Archive → Departments**. Uprevit takes you to the Archive page.

Archiving a department doesn't archive its projects.

## Archiving projects

<Callout type="warn" title="Admin only">
Archiving a project requires **Admin** privileges.
</Callout>

Same flow on the project's page, with exact **project name** confirmation.

## Archiving products

<Callout type="warn" title="Admin only">
Archiving a product requires **Admin** privileges.
</Callout>

1. On `/products`, open the row menu.
2. Choose **Archive**, then click **Archive Product**. Products don't need a typed confirmation.
3. The whole product, with every version, moves to **Archive → Products**. Each version keeps its status (Draft, Submitted, Released, or Obsolete).

A product that is **In Review** can't be archived. End its [approval workflow](/docs/review-outputs/approval-workflows) first. Archived products can't be added to a new workflow.

Everyone sees the **Archive** action. If you aren't an admin, Uprevit asks you to contact an admin.

## Restoring entities

<Callout type="warn" title="Admin only">
Restoring departments, projects, or products requires **Admin** privileges.
</Callout>

1. Go to `/archive` and select the correct tab.
2. Click **Restore** on the row.
3. Confirm in the dialog.

| Entity | After restore |
|--------|----------------|
| **Department** | Back in active lists. |
| **Project** | Back in active lists. |
| **Product** | Back in active lists. Every version keeps the status it had. |

## Archive activity logs

<Callout type="warn" title="Admin only">
Only admins see the **Logs** button on `/archive`.
</Callout>

Click **Logs** to open **Archive Logs** and see who archived or restored departments, projects, and products, and when.

To compare versions of a product, see [Redlines and Versions](/docs/review-outputs/redlines-versions).
