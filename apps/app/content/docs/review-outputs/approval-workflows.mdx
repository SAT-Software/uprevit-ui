---
title: Approval Workflows
description: Send product versions for review, approve or request changes, and release them together.
---

An **approval workflow** groups the latest version of one or more products for review. Approvers are assigned by **Function** (for example Product Team, Quality, or Regulatory). When everyone approves, all versions in the workflow are **Released** together. A workflow is the only way a version becomes Released.

Each workflow gets a permanent number such as `AM-WF-000123`. The letters come from your workspace name.

## Product lifecycle

```text
Draft → Submitted → In Review → Released → Obsolete
                        │
                        └─ Rejected or Cancelled workflow → back to Submitted
```

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Draft** | Being written. New products and new versions start here. |
| **Submitted** | Ready for a workflow. Still editable. The Product Owner, a Contributor, or an admin can return it to Draft before a workflow starts. |
| **In Review** | Inside an active workflow. Still editable; approvers are told about changes. |
| **Released** | Approved and frozen. Create a new version to make changes. |
| **Obsolete** | An older release replaced by a newer one. Kept as read-only history. |

While a newer version is being worked on, the product header shows its status and workflow number, which opens the active workflow, and a **Released vN** link to the current release. In product tables, hover the status or version to open the workflow or the released version.

### Workflow statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Draft** | Being set up. Not started yet. |
| **In Review** | Started. Approvers are deciding. |
| **Ready to Complete** | Everyone approved. Waiting for the Initiator or an admin to complete it. |
| **Completed** | Products were released. |
| **Rejected** | An approver rejected it. Products returned to **Submitted**. |
| **Cancelled** | The Initiator or an admin cancelled it. Products returned to **Submitted**. |

## Submit a product

1. Mark all seven tabs complete so progress shows **100%**.
2. Click **Submit for approval** in the product header and confirm.

The version becomes **Submitted**. Nothing is released yet.

## Create and start a workflow

1. Open **Workflows** in the sidebar and click **Create Workflow**.
2. Enter a **Name** and **Description**, choose a **completion mode**, then click **Create Draft**. You become the workflow's **Initiator**.
   - **Automatic:** releases the products as soon as everyone approves.
   - **Initiator-controlled:** waits for the Initiator or an admin to click **Complete Workflow**.
3. On the **Products** tab, add one or more products. You can add Draft products, but each one must be **Submitted** and 100% complete before the workflow starts.
4. On the **Approvals** tab, add approvers:
   - **Product Team:** at least one owner or contributor of each product.
   - **Other Functions:** at least one more Function, such as Quality. Type the Function name first, then choose the approver.
5. Check the **Ready to start?** list, then click **Start Workflow**.

The completion mode starts from your workspace default, which admins set in **Settings → Workspace**. While the workflow is a draft, you can still edit it, remove products or approvers, or click **Delete Draft**.

**Ready to start?** checks that:

- There's at least 1 product.
- Every product is the latest version, **Submitted**, and 100% complete.
- No product is in another active workflow.
- Every product has a Product Team approver.
- At least 1 other Function is assigned.
- Every approver is an active member with a Function.
- Neither the Product Owner nor the Initiator is the only approver.

After start, the products, approvers, and completion mode are locked. The products move to **In Review**. Every approver is [notified](/docs/review-outputs/notifications), and so are Product Owners who aren't approvers.

## Review and decide

Approvers open the workflow from **Workflows → My Tasks** or from their notification.

| Decision | What happens |
|----------|--------------|
| **Approve** | Records approval for your assignment. You can add a comment. |
| **Request Changes** | Keeps the workflow open. Add a reason for one product or the whole workflow. Approvals from other approvers keep counting. If you had approved earlier, your approval stops counting. Click **Approve** once your request is addressed. |
| **Reject** | Ends the whole workflow without release. Every product returns to **Submitted**. A reason is required. |

You can't approve while your own change request is still open.

Use the **Discussion** tab to comment on one product or on the whole workflow. Filter it by **All**, **Requests**, or **Comments**. The Discussion tab opens when the workflow starts. The Initiator, admins, approvers, and the product teams can comment while the workflow is active. Comments don't send notifications. Only change requests and their answers do.

### Attach images

Comments and change requests can include up to **4 images**, each up to **10 MB**, in PNG, JPEG, WebP, or GIF format. Use **Attach images** or paste images into the text box, then wait for uploads to finish before posting. Click an attached image to open it.

### Address requested changes

The Product Owner or a Contributor edits the product. The Initiator, or an owner or contributor of the scoped product, can then click **Mark Addressed** with a short note. For a request about the whole workflow, an owner or contributor of any included product can address it. The current assigned approver is notified and still needs to decide; addressing a request does not count as approval.

## Change notices and Approve again

In Review versions can still be edited. When someone saves a change to reviewed product content, approvers get a **change notice**. Each approver gets one notice per decision, so repeated saves don't keep notifying them. The person who made the change isn't notified. Approvals are kept and the workflow status does not change.

An approver whose approval came before the latest change sees **Approve again**. It records a fresh approval against the current content. It is optional and never required to complete the workflow.

## Replace approvers and send reminders

While the workflow is **In Review**, only the Initiator can **replace** a pending approver with a reason. Both the new and the replaced approver are notified. Decisions already made stay with the original person. If an approver leaves the workspace or the product team, their assignment shows **Needs replacement** and the Initiator is notified.

The Initiator can click **Send Reminder** to notify chosen approvers in the app and by email, with an optional message. Approvers who haven't decided, or who requested changes, are selected by default.

## Complete, cancel, and history

- **Complete:** in Initiator-controlled mode, the Initiator or an admin completes a **Ready to Complete** workflow. All products are released together; any previous release becomes **Obsolete**.
- **Cancel:** the Initiator or an admin can cancel an active workflow with a reason. Products return to **Submitted**. A cancelled workflow can't be restarted. Create a new one to approve the products.
- **History:** every decision, change, replacement, and reminder is kept in the workflow's **History** tab.

A product page's **Workflows** button lists every workflow that product was in.

## Find workflows

- **Workflows** has three tabs: **All Workflows**, **Created by Me**, and **My Tasks**. Search by number or name and filter by status or product.
- **Advanced search** finds workflows with up to 10 conditions joined with **AND** or **OR**, such as *Status is In Review AND Function contains Quality*. You can search by number, name, status, product, Initiator, approver, Function, completion mode, and milestone dates.
- Save a search to load it again later. Saved searches stay in your browser.

<Callout title="Not an electronic signature">
Workflow approvals are an internal approval record. They are not legally binding electronic signatures and do not replace your regulatory or QMS sign-off process.
</Callout>
