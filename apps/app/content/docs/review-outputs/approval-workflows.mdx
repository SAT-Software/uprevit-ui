---
title: Approval Workflows
description: Send product versions for review, approve or request changes, and release them together.
---

An **approval workflow** packages the latest version of one or more products for review. Approvers are assigned by **Function** (for example Product Team, Quality, or Regulatory). When everyone approves, all versions in the package are **Released** together. A workflow is the only way a version becomes Released.

Each workflow gets a permanent number such as `AM-WF-000123`. The letters come from your workspace name.

## Product lifecycle

```text
Draft → Submitted → In Review → Released → Obsolete
                        │
                        └─ Rejected or Cancelled workflow → back to Submitted
```

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Draft** | Being written. New products and new versions start here. |
| **Submitted** | Ready for a workflow. Still editable. The Product Owner or a Contributor can return it to Draft before a workflow starts. |
| **In Review** | Inside an active workflow. Still editable; approvers are told about changes. |
| **Released** | Approved and frozen. Create a new version to make changes. |
| **Obsolete** | An older release replaced by a newer one. Kept as read-only history. |

Product tables and the product header show the working version and the released version on one row, for example **v4 In Review · Released v3**, with a link to the active workflow.

## Submit a product

1. Mark all seven tabs complete so progress shows **100%**.
2. Click **Submit for approval** in the product header and confirm.

The version becomes **Submitted**. Nothing is released yet.

## Create and start a workflow

1. Open **Workflows** in the sidebar and click **Create Workflow**.
2. Add one or more Submitted products.
3. Add approvers:
   - **Product Team:** at least one owner or contributor of each product.
   - **Other Functions:** at least one more Function, such as Quality. Type the Function name when you add the approver.
4. Choose a **completion mode**:
   - **Automatic:** releases the products as soon as everyone approves.
   - **Initiator-controlled:** waits for the Initiator or an admin to click **Complete Workflow**.
5. Check the **Ready to start?** list, then click **Start Workflow**.

After start, the products, approvers, and completion mode are locked. The products move to **In Review** and every approver is notified.

## Review and decide

Approvers open the workflow from **Workflows → My Tasks** or from their notification.

| Decision | What happens |
|----------|--------------|
| **Approve** | Records approval for your assignment. |
| **Request Changes** | Keeps the workflow open. Add a reason for one product or the whole package. Your earlier approval stops counting. |
| **Reject** | Ends the whole workflow without release. Every product returns to **Submitted**. A reason is required. |

Use the **Discussion** tab to comment on one product or on the whole package.

### Address requested changes

The Product Owner or a Contributor edits the product, then clicks **Mark Addressed** with a short note. The approver who asked is notified. Addressing a request does not count as approval.

## Change notices and Approve again

In Review versions can still be edited. When someone saves a change to reviewed product content, every approver gets one **change notice**. Approvals are kept and the workflow status does not change.

An approver whose approval came before the latest change sees **Approve again**. It records a fresh approval against the current content. It is optional and never required to complete the workflow.

## Replace approvers and send reminders

The Initiator or an admin can **replace** a pending approver with a reason. Decisions already made stay with the original person. If an approver leaves the workspace or the product team, their assignment shows **Needs replacement** and the Initiator is notified.

The Initiator can click **Send Reminder** to notify chosen approvers, with an optional message. Pending approvers are selected by default.

## Complete, cancel, and history

- **Complete:** in Initiator-controlled mode, the Initiator or an admin completes a **Ready to Complete** workflow. All products are released together; any previous release becomes **Obsolete**.
- **Cancel:** the Initiator or an admin can cancel an active workflow with a reason. Products return to **Submitted**.
- **History:** every decision, change, replacement, and reminder is kept in the workflow's **History** tab.

A product page's **Workflows** button lists every workflow that product was in.

## Find workflows

- **Workflows** lists all workflows, the ones you created, and your tasks. Search by number or name and filter by status or product.
- **Advanced search** finds workflows with up to 10 conditions joined with **AND** or **OR**, such as *Status is In Review AND Function contains Quality*. You can search by number, name, status, product, Initiator, approver, Function, completion mode, and milestone dates.
- Save a search to load it again later. Saved searches stay in your browser.

<Callout title="Not an electronic signature">
Workflow approvals are an internal approval record. They are not legally binding electronic signatures or a validated Part 11 record.
</Callout>
