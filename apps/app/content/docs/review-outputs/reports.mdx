**Reports** (`/reports`) lets you search products workspace-wide using conditions on root product fields and on data from each documentation tab.

<DocsVideo videoKey="reports-analytics.reports" title="Reports walkthrough" />

## Query builder

Click **Add First Condition** to start, then **Add Another Condition** for more. You can combine up to **10 conditions** with **AND** or **OR**.

| Piece | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Category** | The product area to search, such as Product, Dates, Languages, Barcodes, or Label Tags. |
| **Field** | Attribute within that category. |
| **Operator** | Depends on the field: text fields match exactly or by part, numbers and counts compare with =, ≠, &gt;, ≥, &lt;, or ≤, dates match on, before, or after a day, and list fields such as **Presence on Labels** match any or all of several values. |
| **Value** | Text, number, date, a pick list, or several values, depending on the field. |

Text matches ignore upper and lower case. Click **Run Query** to see the results. Click **Clear all conditions** to start over.

### Categories

- **Product** — name, description, PPN, department, project, status, version, **Latest Version**, and **Archived**.
- **Dates** — created, target, completion, released, obsoleted, and archived dates.
- **Team & Workflow** — owner, contributors (by name or email), whether the product is in an active workflow, released through a workflow, or a legacy release.
- **Progress** — whether a given tab is complete, and how many tabs are complete.
- **Product Information** and **Custom Fields** — market, country of origin, commercial/clinical, device class, Basic UDI-DI, and your custom fields.
- **Compliance Information** and **Languages** — standards, and label languages by name, code, or country.
- **Symbols**, **Schematics**, **Barcodes**, and **Other Components** — each item's name or type, description, and presence on labels, plus symbol text and standard library fields, and barcode data and count.
- **Label Components** and **Label Tags** — component type, number, label type, dimensions, print direction, and label tag names, types, and legend text.

Each list category also has a **Number of …** field, for example **Number of Barcodes ≥ 2** or **Number of Symbols = 0**.

When conditions on the same list category are joined by **AND**, they must all match the same item. For example, **Barcode Type equals GS1-128** AND **Presence on Labels includes any of Pouch** finds products with a GS1-128 barcode on the pouch.

Every version of a product is a separate result. Add **Latest Version is Yes** to see only the newest version, or filter **Version ≥ 2**. Reports include archived products unless you add a condition on **Archived**.

## Saved queries

Click **Save current query** to name and save your conditions. Click **Load a saved query** to reuse or delete one. Saved queries are stored in your workspace, so they follow you to any device; tick **Share with everyone in this workspace** to let teammates load them too. Only the person who saved a query can delete it.

## Results

**Query Results** lists matching products, 10 per page, with PPN, product name, project, department, status, and version. Click **Columns** to show more, such as owner, dates, market, device class, or languages. Your column choice is remembered in this browser. Click a row to open the product.

When there are results, click **PDF** or **Excel** in the results header to export them. See [Exports](/docs/review-outputs/exports).

## Report export jobs

Click **Exports** on `/reports` to open **Report Exports**:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Queued** | Waiting to start. |
| **Processing** | The file is being created. |
| **Ready** | Click the download icon to save the file. |
| **Failed** | The export didn't finish. The error is shown on the job. |

A message appears when an export is ready, even if you've moved to another page.

## Permissions

Reports are available to all workspace members. No admin-only gate applies to building or running queries. Only Admins and Users can export results.
