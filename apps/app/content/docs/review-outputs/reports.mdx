**Reports** (`/reports`) lets you search products workspace-wide using conditions on root product fields and on data from each documentation tab.

<DocsVideo videoKey="reports-analytics.overview" title="Reports and Analytics overview" cropPadding />

## Query builder

Click **Add First Condition** to start, then **Add Another Condition** for more. You can combine up to **10 conditions** with **AND** or **OR**.

| Piece | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Category** | The product area to search: Product, Product Information, Compliance Information, Label Components, or Symbols & Graphics. |
| **Field** | Attribute within that category. |
| **Operator** | Equals, Does not equal, Contains, Does not contain, Exists (has value), Does not exist (empty), Contains any, or Contains all, depending on the field. |
| **Value** | Text, select, or multi-select depending on field type. |

Click **Run Query** to see the results. Click **Clear all conditions** to start over.

### Example categories

- **Product** — status, product name, PPN, version, and whether the product is archived.
- **Product Information** — market, country of origin, commercial/clinical, Basic UDI, and related metadata.
- **Compliance Information** — standards and languages.
- **Label Components / Symbols & Graphics** — list fields you can check for a value or for specific entries.

Reports include archived products unless you add a condition on **Archived**.

## Saved queries

Click **Save current query** to name and save your conditions. Click **Load a saved query** to reuse or delete one. Saved queries are stored in your browser on this device, so they aren't shared with other users.

## Results

**Query Results** lists matching products, 10 per page, with PPN, product name, project, department, status, and version. Click a row to open the product.

When there are results, click **PDF** or **Excel** in the results header to export them. See [Exports](/docs/review-outputs/exports).

## Report export jobs

Click **Exports** on `/reports` to open **Report Exports**:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Queued** | Waiting to start. |
| **Processing** | The file is being created. |
| **Ready** | Click the download icon to save the file. |
| **Failed** | The export didn't finish. The error is shown on the job. |

A message appears when an export is ready, even if you've moved to another page.

## Permissions

Reports are available to all workspace members. No admin-only gate applies to building or running queries.
