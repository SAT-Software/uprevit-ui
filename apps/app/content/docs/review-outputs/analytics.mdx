**Analytics** (`/analytics`) shows how work is moving through your workspace. Open it from **Insights → Analytics** in the sidebar. Every workspace member can view it.

Use the filters at the top to choose a period (**Last 30 days** to **Last 12 months**, default **Last 90 days**), a department and a project. Every card and chart follows the filters.

Click **In progress**, **Released** or **Overdue**, a chart segment, a calendar day, a month in **Created vs released** or a tab in **What blocks submission** to open the products behind it. Click a department to open its page.

## Summary cards

| Card | What it shows |
|------|---------------|
| **In progress** | Products in Draft, Submitted or In review. |
| **Released** | Versions released in the selected period. |
| **On-time rate** | Share of versions released in the period on or before their target date. |
| **Median time to release** | Median days from creating a version to releasing it, for versions released in the period. |
| **Pending approvals** | Products waiting on approvers in active reviews, counted once per approver. |
| **Overdue** | Unreleased products past their target date. |

## Charts

- **Pipeline by stage** shows how many products are in Draft, Submitted, In review and Released.
- **Due-date calendar** shows unreleased products by target date, from the start of the selected period to eight weeks ahead. Days before today are overdue.
- **Pending approvals** shows how many products are waiting on each approver.
- **Created vs released** compares versions created and versions released each month.
- **What blocks submission** shows how many drafts still have each of the seven product tabs incomplete. A draft can count under several tabs.
- **Departments by stage** shows each department's products split by stage.
