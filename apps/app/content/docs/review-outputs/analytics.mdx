**Analytics** (`/analytics`) shows your workspace's products as numbers and charts. Open it from **Insights → Analytics** in the sidebar. Every workspace member can view it.

<DocsVideo videoKey="reports-analytics.overview" title="Reports and Analytics overview" />

## Summary cards

| Card | What it counts |
|------|----------------|
| **Total Products** | Active products in the workspace. |
| **Draft** | Products whose latest version is in Draft. |
| **Released** | Products whose latest version is released. |
| **Archived** | Products that have been archived. |
| **Overdue** | Draft products past their target date. |

## Charts

- **Products Over Time** counts each product once, on the date it was last created or changed. Choose a range from **Last 7 days** to **Last 1 year**. The default is **Last 1 month**.
- **Products by Department** shows the five departments with the most products.
- **Products by Project** shows the five projects with the most products.

<Callout type="info" title="Large workspaces">
  **Draft**, **Released**, **Overdue**, and the charts are based on up to 100 products. **Total Products** and **Archived** always count every product.
</Callout>
