The **Compliance Information** tab documents regulatory standards and the languages used on packaging or labeling for this product version.

<DocsVideo videoKey="product.compliance-tab" title="Compliance Information tab walkthrough" cropPadding />

## Sections

### Compliance standards

Each standard is a card with:

| Field | Required | Notes |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **Standard Number / Regulation** | Yes | Choose **Select from list** to search the built-in catalog of standards, regulations, and audit programs, or **Enter custom** to type one (at least 3 characters). |
| **Description** | No | Filled in for you when you pick from the list, and you can still edit it. Up to 500 characters. |

When you pick from the list, **Selected Details** shows the standard's type, category, and scope.

**Actions:** Add, edit, or delete standards while the version is in **Draft**, **Submitted**, or **In Review**.

### Languages

Manage the set of languages for labeling:

| Field | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| **Language code, name, country** | Selected in **Manage Product Languages**. |

In **Manage Product Languages** you can:

- Search by code, language, or country.
- Pick languages one by one.
- Add a whole market group at once, such as Pan-European, DACH, Nordics, Latin America, or Gulf / Arabic.
- Use **Select Visible**, **Clear Visible**, or **Clear All**.

Click **Save Languages** to replace the product's language list with your selection.

## Editing workflow

1. Open **Compliance Information** from the product sidebar.
2. Under **Compliance Standards**, click **Add Standard**. On an existing standard, use the edit or delete icon. Deleting asks you to confirm.
3. Under **Languages**, click **Manage Languages**, select languages, then click **Save Languages**.
4. When finished, hover the progress in the product header and click **Mark Complete**.

Released and Obsolete versions cannot add, edit, or delete standards or languages until a new draft version exists. Only the Product Owner, Contributors, and admins can edit.

## Tab completion

Hover the progress in the product header and click **Mark Complete** to mark **Compliance Information** complete.

## Version comparison (redline)

In compare mode, standards and languages show per-item **NEW**, **DEL**, and **MOD** badges with inline old/new values.

See **[Redlines and Versions](/docs/review-outputs/redlines-versions)** for how to enable and read redline views across tabs.
