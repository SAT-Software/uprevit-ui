**Product Information** is the first documentation tab in the product workflow. It holds identifying metadata, scheduling dates, regulatory geography, device classification, custom fields, and links to related **source file** folders. For all seven tabs and the shared header tools, start with [Product Overview](/docs/product-documentation/product-overview).

## Opening the tab

From the product list, click a product row—or use the sidebar **Product Information** link when viewing any product.

## Fields on the page

### Product details

Click **Update** at the top of the page to open **Edit Product Details**:

| Field | Required | Notes |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **Product Name** | Yes | Also shown in the product header. |
| **Description** | No | Longer narrative than the list description. |
| **Target Date** | No | Planned completion date. |

### Product information

Click **Update Product Info** to open **Edit Product Information**:

| Field | Required | Notes |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **Market / Geography** | Yes* | Choose from preset markets or enter a custom value. |
| **Country of Origin** | Yes* | Preset country or custom entry. |
| **OEM / Contract manufacturer** | Yes | |
| **Commercial / Clinical** | Yes | Free text, for example Commercial or Clinical. |
| **Manufacturing Location** | No | |
| **Class of Device** | No | Preset device classes grouped by regulation, or custom. |
| **Basic UDI** | No | Unique Device Identification (when applicable). |

\*Required means you must provide either a preset selection or custom text.

Click **Save Changes** to save. Changes apply to this product version.

### Status and dates

Hover the status badge at the top of the page to see:

- **Status** — Draft, Submitted, In Review, Released, or Obsolete.
- **Target date** and **actual completion date**. The actual date is set when you submit the version.
- **Created by** and **Updated by**, with dates.
- **Linked source files** — folders in **Source Files** tied to this product. Click one to open it.

### Custom fields

Add organization-specific label/value pairs. Click **Manage Custom Fields**:

- On **Add New Field**, enter a **Label** and **Value** (both required), then click **Add Field(s)**.
- On **Manage Existing Fields**, change a field and click **Update**, or click **Delete** and confirm.

## Who can edit

Only the Product Owner, Contributors, and admins can edit. When status is **Released** or **Obsolete**, edit actions are disabled until you create a [new version](/docs/review-outputs/redlines-versions).

Click **Workflows** to see every approval workflow this product was in.

## Tab completion

Hover the progress in the product header and click **Mark Complete** when Product Information is finished. This counts toward the seven-tab progress bar required for submit.

## Activity logs

Admins can click **Logs** to see a detailed change history for this product.

## Version comparison (redline)

To see what changed in Product Information between versions, click **Compare** in the product header. Field-level highlights and custom-field badges (NEW / DEL / MOD) appear on this tab.

For the full comparison workflow, see **[Redlines and Versions](/docs/review-outputs/redlines-versions)**—that page is the canonical guide; this tab does not duplicate those steps.
