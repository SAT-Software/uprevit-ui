## 0.10.0 — Analytics and multi-sheet workbooks

<DocsChangelogGroup type="new">

- [Analytics](/docs/review-outputs/analytics) is redesigned with the product pipeline, due dates, created vs released, what blocks submission, and pending approvals. Select any tile or chart segment to see the products behind it.
- Workbooks can have several sheets. Add, rename, reorder, and delete sheets, and import or export them all with Excel.
- New walkthrough videos for Workspace, Operational Parameters, Notifications, Exports, Archives, and Bookmarks.

</DocsChangelogGroup>

<DocsChangelogGroup type="improved">

- Product pages load faster.
- Workbook editing is smoother, with better keyboard support, Word paste, conditional formatting, and redlines.
- Bookmarks follow the latest product version.
- The product team can only be changed on the latest version.

</DocsChangelogGroup>

<DocsChangelogGroup type="fixed">

- Image comparisons match the right rows and show when a comparison fails.
- Tall and wide label images stay inside the editor.
- Boolean filters and custom-field activity values display correctly.

</DocsChangelogGroup>

## 0.8.0 — Approval workflows and Beta

<DocsChangelogGroup type="new">

- [Approval workflows](/docs/review-outputs/approval-workflows) let you review and release one or more products together.
- Approvers can request changes, discuss them per product, and attach images.
- **Approve again** lets approvers confirm their approval after reviewed content changes.
- Initiators can replace approvers and send reminders. Unavailable approvers are flagged.
- Advanced workflow search supports up to 10 conditions and saved searches.
- [Product Owners and Contributors](/docs/getting-started/products#product-team) decide who can edit each product.
- [Notifications](/docs/review-outputs/notifications) in the app and by email keep product teams and approvers informed.
- Each product shows its released version, its active workflow, and every workflow it was in.
- [Ask AI](/docs/ask-ai) answers questions about the documentation.
- [Product Specifications](/docs/product-documentation/product-specifications) and Operational Parameters can import workbooks with several sheets.
- Profiles have a **Unit** field, and label components have **Print Direction**.

</DocsChangelogGroup>

<DocsChangelogGroup type="improved">

- Uprevit is now in **Beta**, and **Workflows** is marked **New** in the sidebar.
- Products move through Draft, Submitted, In Review, Released, and Obsolete. Submitting prepares a version; a workflow releases it.
- Version menus show working, released, and obsolete versions, with links to their workflows.
- Product PDFs include Print Direction and standard symbol images.

</DocsChangelogGroup>

<DocsChangelogGroup type="fixed">

- Product exports include table edits you just saved.
- Ask AI chat history is kept separate for each account.
- Only admins see **Invite Users**.
- Approvers removed from a product team or the workspace can no longer record decisions.

</DocsChangelogGroup>

</DocsChangelogRelease>
