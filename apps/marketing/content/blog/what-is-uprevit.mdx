Ask a labeling team where the latest label for a product lives and you will often get a pause. Then a few different answers. The artwork is in one folder. The spec values are in a spreadsheet someone emailed last week. The approval is somewhere in an inbox.

We built Uprevit because that pause is where most labeling problems start.

## The problem with labels today

A medical device label looks simple. A name, a few symbols, a barcode, some text. But behind every label sits a lot of data. Device class and markets. The standards you follow. The symbols you use and why. Dimensions, materials, storage conditions. Translations.

Most teams keep that data spread across tools that were never built for it:

- Spreadsheets for specifications, often with names like "specs final v2"
- Word or PDF files for label drafts
- Email threads for review and sign off
- Shared drives for artwork and source files

Each tool works fine on its own. The trouble starts when they need to agree with each other. A value changes in the spreadsheet but not on the label. A reviewer approves an old draft. An auditor asks who changed the storage temperature and when, and nobody can answer quickly.

None of this is anyone's fault. It is just what happens when the record of a product is split across ten places.

## What Uprevit is

Uprevit is labeling management software for medical device teams. It gives every product one structured record and keeps that record under version control, from the first draft to release.

Instead of hunting through folders, you open the product and everything is there, organized in seven tabs:

- **Product Information** for names, markets, Basic UDI and device class
- **Compliance Information** for the standards and languages that apply
- **Label Components** for the physical labels, tags and panels
- **Symbols & Graphics** for symbols, barcodes and schematics
- **Product Specifications** and **Operational Parameters** for the numbers, in a spreadsheet style grid
- **Label Tags** for annotated label artwork

Products sit inside projects, and projects sit inside departments, so even a large portfolio stays easy to browse.

## Versions you can trust

Every product moves through a clear lifecycle: Draft, Submitted, In Review and Released. Once a version is released, it is read only. If something needs to change, you start a new version. The old one stays in the history, so you can always see exactly what was approved and when.

You can also compare versions side by side. The redline view shows what changed, so reviewers do not have to spot differences by eye.

## Reviews without the email chain

Approvals happen inside Uprevit too. You send one or more product versions into an approval workflow, assign approvers and track progress in one place. Each approver can approve or request changes. If content changes while a version is in review, approvers get a notice, so nobody signs off on something they have not seen.

When the workflow completes, the version is released and any earlier release is marked obsolete. No more guessing which draft is the real one.

## Getting data out

A record is only useful if you can share it. Uprevit lets you export a full product as a PDF, export specification tables and build reports that search products across tabs. That makes it easier to answer audit questions, send data to a printer or check that a symbol is used the same way across a product line.

## Who it is for

Uprevit is built for the people who own labeling at medical device companies:

- Labeling and regulatory affairs specialists who maintain label content
- Quality teams who need a clean history for audits
- Engineers and product managers who supply specification data
- Managers who want to see where every product stands

If you have ever rebuilt a label from three different files, it is probably for you.

## What it is not

Uprevit is not a design tool, and it does not replace your artwork software. It does not make regulatory decisions either. It gives your team one reliable place to hold labeling data, review it and show what was approved. The judgment stays with you.
